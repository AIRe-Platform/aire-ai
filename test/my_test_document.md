# Recipe for Strawberry Kilju

Ingredients:

- 1 litre of strawberries
- 2 kilograms of sugar
- ½ cube of yeast

Instructions:

- Fill a kettle half way with water
- Add strawberries
- Boil until the color and flavor have dissolved into the water
- Add sugar, boil and stir until fully dissolved
- Move the concoction into a fermentation vessel
- Move the hot liquid around the vessel to sterilize it
- Add cold water until the vessel is almost full
- The liquid should be hand-warm, if not, let it cool down
- Add yeast and close the vessel
- Move the vessel somewhere warm
- Let the kilju ferment a couple weeks
- Check the taste
- Bottle the kilju (optional)
- Drink the kilju
